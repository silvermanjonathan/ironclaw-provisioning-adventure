One more thing stands between you and the elevator. The updater is blinking: a new build is waiting, and the note beside it describes, politely and in the past tense, the kind of bug it fixes.

Applying it costs twenty minutes and a restart — tonight, on launch eve. The quarter-end freeze says nothing changes this week, and the freeze has a signature on it. The manual has one line about this, and the line is not long: keep current.

Two rules, one evening. The calendar will side with one of them.

If you take the twenty minutes tonight, Turn to page 60. If you let the freeze win and defer it, Turn to page 61.
