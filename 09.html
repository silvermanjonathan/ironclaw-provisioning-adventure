The settlement feed carries the money data. How should it reach Bursar?

Jonah, the friendly engineer from the payment processor, shows you three ways. One: Bursar calls out and asks for the data, and nothing outside can ever call in. It is a few minutes slower. Two: one tunnel with one locked door — but the two official manuals disagree about whether the door locks itself. Three: open Bursar to the whole internet, "just for launch week."

Jonah sighs. "Everyone does it," he says. That is exactly what worries you.

If Bursar calls out and nothing is allowed in, Turn to page 10. If you build the one locked tunnel, Turn to page 11. If you open Bursar to the internet for now, Turn to page 12.
