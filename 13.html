Now the big one. How much power should Bursar's money key have?

Look only: Bursar sees everything and moves nothing. Prepare: Bursar builds each payment, but a person must press the button. By itself: Bursar can move money with no person at all, up to a limit.

Marisol looks up from her desk. "I have been the button for six years," she says. "Tell me if I've stopped."

If the key can look but never move money, Turn to page 14. If Bursar prepares and a person still presses the button, Turn to page 15. If Bursar can move money by itself, under a limit, Turn to page 16.
