You did nearly everything right. You even installed the add-on with reduced powers, just as the manual says. Here is what happens anyway.

A payment has been built for an account nobody knows.

It stops in Marisol's queue. She reads the name twice, does not know it, and picks up the phone. That is what the queue is for. The loss stays small: one key replaced, one hard phone call, one long afternoon.

It cannot go out at all, because the key cannot move money. What you lose instead is an afternoon spent proving that nothing left.

It goes out — because going out is what "by itself" means — and the calls to get it back begin before lunch.

The loss is small. Small enough to shrug off. Do not shrug. A loss you shrug off changes nothing. Write the report as if it had been a hundred times bigger.
