Marisol appears with her phone. "Can I just ask Bursar things? From the queue, from lunch, from the bus?" Behind her, Theo wonders aloud whether Aldergate's coordinator would like a handle too. It would save so many emails.

The manual's switch for this has three positions, and it ships on the careful one: a phone must shake hands with the desk, once, before Bursar will listen to it. Or you can write a short list of names yourself. Or you can open the handle, so that anyone who finds it can ask.

None of this opens a new door in the wall. The question is not how messages travel. The question is whose messages count as work.
