You did not make a choice, or follow any instruction, but somehow you are here — on the page that no page points to.

In the week this book never shows, you ask Theo for seven more days, and you get them. Noor stays late with you before her trip. Together you change four settings, shrink one key, trim one list, tune one channel, and move one file out of the home folder.

Nothing about that week is exciting. The notes fit on one page. And no ending waits for it — because when a disaster is prevented, there is nothing left to show. That is why weeks like this are so hard to ask for, and so easy to grant.

You found this page by reading the book instead of trusting it. Keep that.
